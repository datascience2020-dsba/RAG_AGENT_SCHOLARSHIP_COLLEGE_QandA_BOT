## **About the Scholarship**

At Intoxalock, our mission is to keep roads safer for everyone by preventing drunk driving. In support of that mission, we offer this $3,000 scholarship to a student who is currently enrolled, or has been accepted for enrollment, at any accredited two- or four-year U.S. college, university, or trade/vocational program.

Students across all fields of study are encouraged to apply. The award is non-renewable and can be used for tuition, housing, books, or any other education-related expenses.

**How Finalists Are Selected**

Selection is based on a combination of internal review scores and public social media voting. After the submission window closes, our internal review team will evaluate all entries against the judging criteria. Finalists will then be featured in a public contest hosted on Intoxalock’s Facebook and Instagram pages, where the community can show their support through likes, shares, and comments. Intoxalock will evaluate overall social engagement alongside internal scores to determine the final winner.
