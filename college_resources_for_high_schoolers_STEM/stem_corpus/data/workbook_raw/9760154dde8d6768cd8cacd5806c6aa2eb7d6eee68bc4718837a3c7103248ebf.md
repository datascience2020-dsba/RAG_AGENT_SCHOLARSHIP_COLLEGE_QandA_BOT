# Chick-fil-A Community Scholars

---

Through the Chick-fil-A™ Community Scholars program, Chick-fil-A, Inc. helps pay for the education of leaders in our communities who want to continue learning, while also investing in their leadership development.

Applications close November 3, 2026

## Apply to become a 2027 Chick-fil-A Community Scholar

[Apply now](https://scholarshipamerica.org/scholarship/cfacommunityscholars/)

[Meet the Scholars](https://www.chick-fil-a.com/community-scholars/meet-the-scholars) [Application Tips](https://www.chick-fil-a.com/community-scholars/application-tips)

## Developing future leaders

Every year, thirteen (13) $25,000 scholarships are awarded to students based on academic success, community service and financial need. The Chick-fil-A Community Scholars also participate in the Chick-fil-A Scholars Program, a one-year engagement that includes mentoring and leadership development alongside our $25k Remarkable Futures Scholars. Through this program, we recognize remarkable individuals who share our dedication to caring for others and caring for their communities. It is our pleasure to support the academic journeys of these future leaders.

## At a glance

**13**Community Scholars every year

**$25K**awarded to each recipient

**1** year-long leadership development and mentorship program

## Relieving educational expenses

Awards can be used for tuition, fees, books and education-related expenses incurred at any accredited college, university or vocational/technical school. Scholarship checks are made payable to the school and cannot be applied toward student loans or refunded to students. In addition to the scholarship, these students participate in a year-long program focused on leadership development and mentoring.
