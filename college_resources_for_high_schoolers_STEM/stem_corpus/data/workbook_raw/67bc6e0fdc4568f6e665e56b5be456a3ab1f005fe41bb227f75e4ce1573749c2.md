## Red Egg Marketing Scholarship

At Red Egg, we believe that higher education is a key part of a successful career. Unfortunately, the high costs of pursuing a degree often prevent many students from continuing their education. To help reduce the financial burden, we have created the Red Egg Marketing Scholarship, available to anyone pursuing a career in a creative or marketing-related field.

## About the Red Egg Marketing Scholarship

The Red Egg Marketing Scholarship is awarded semi-annually, providing two marketing scholarships of $2,500 each per year (one per semester!). We believe in helping students get ahead, which is why we want to help students with their expensive tuition or educational costs.

## Red Egg Marketing Scholarship Application FAQs

**Q**: **How much is the scholarship?**
**A**: We award two scholarships of $2,500 each per year. One per semester!

**Q: How are the funds distributed?**

**A**: The $2,500 scholarship award is distributed directly to the financial aid office of the winner’s accredited college, university, or vocational-technical school.

**Q**: **When is the next application deadline?**

**A**: Deadlines are October 31 (for the Fall Award) and April 30 (for the Spring Award).

**Q**: **Can I use the money for books or supplies?**

**A**: Yes. The funds are paid to the winner’s school and applied to tuition, fees, or other educational costs.

**Q**: **Do I have to be a specific major?**

**A**: No. You must simply be pursuing a career in a creative or marketing-related field (e.g., communication, web development, graphic design).

**Q**: **Is the scholarship renewable?**

**A**: The scholarship is not automatically renewable, but past recipients are welcome to reapply in a subsequent cycle.
