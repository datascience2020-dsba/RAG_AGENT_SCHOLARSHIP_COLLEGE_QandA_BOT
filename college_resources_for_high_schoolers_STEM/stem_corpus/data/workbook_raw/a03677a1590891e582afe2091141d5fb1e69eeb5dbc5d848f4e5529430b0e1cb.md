# Youth Awards Application

![](../assets/images/bg-decor-10-new.png)

#### About The Youth Awards

The Youth Awards were founded in 1998 by the Hispanic Heritage Foundation. Now entering their 28th year, the Youth Awards honor high school seniors who excel in the classroom and community and for their excellence in various categories including: Community Service, Engineering, Entrepreneurship, Green and Journalism and Media, with additional categories being added throughout the awards season.

Gold, Silver and Bronze recipients are selected in each category and receive a one-time grant to fund their college education or to fund a community service effort that tackles a social issue.

Once selected, recipients will be celebrated in virtual ceremonies and will become part of our broader HHF network of 200,000 professionals and fulfill our unique cycle of leadership where we meet America's priorities in the classroom and workforce.

The Youth Awards program is the main pipeline to Hispanic Heritage Foundation's (HHF) system of talent that prepares and positions young leaders through the award-winning Latinos On Fast Track (LOFT) program as they transition from high school, to college, to graduate school, and into their careers. Follow LOFT on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/hispanicheritage/), [X](https://twitter.com/HHFoundation) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/hispanicheritage/?hl=en).

#### Application Deadline: November 1st, 2026

#### Youth Awards Application

Eligibility

* Be currently enrolled in high school or home school program and graduating in the spring of 2027
* Must have a minimum unweighted 3.0 GPA on a 4.0 scale or 7.5 on a 10.0 scale
* Must enroll at an accredited higher education institution in 2027-2028
* If selected, attendance to the virtual awards ceremony is mandatory

Categories

* Community Service
* Engineering
* Entrepreneurship
* Green
* Journalism and Media

Regions

Open to all US states and territories

### HAZ LA U

![HAZ LA U](../images/HAZLAU.png)

Colgate-Palmolive is proud to sponsor the Youth Awards through the “Haz La U” (Make the U) college grant program for high school seniors. Colgate-Palmolive believes that a higher education is a path to a brighter future and is dedicated to helping students get there.

Have you focused on academic excellence, dedicated service to your community, and demonstrated leadership? Your effort could be rewarded by receiving one of 31 educational grants with Colgate providing $100,000 in total support.
