# Scholarships

Funding education for promising students across the DMV since 1997.

Since 1997, the Sejong Scholarship Foundation of America has awarded need- and merit-based
scholarships to high-school and college students in the Washington D.C., Maryland, and Virginia
area — over **$745,000 to 747+ students** to date. Each year we recognize students
who excel academically but face financial need, helping them pursue their education and give back
to their communities.

Our founders — first-generation Korean-American immigrants — knew firsthand that education is the
surest path to opportunity. They started this fund so that students who work hard but lack the means
would never have to abandon their dreams for financial reasons.

Today, second- and third-generation volunteers carry that mission forward, reviewing every
application personally and celebrating each recipient at our annual award ceremony.
