## About the Friedman & Simon, L.L.P. Academic Scholarship

“We believe in providing resources to children and young people so they have every advantage available to become successful adults,” said [Jericho personal injury attorney](https://www.friedmansimon.com/jericho-personal-injury-lawyer/) Edward Friedman, a partner at the firm.

As part of that goal to supply young people with resources for success, Friedman & Simon, L.L.P. created a scholarship to help a devoted and deserving student attend college.

“We are all heavily invested in the lives and educations of young people,” said attorney Roger Simon, a partner at the firm. “It is our hope that this scholarship helps an impressive student get a college education that they use to make a difference in the world.”
