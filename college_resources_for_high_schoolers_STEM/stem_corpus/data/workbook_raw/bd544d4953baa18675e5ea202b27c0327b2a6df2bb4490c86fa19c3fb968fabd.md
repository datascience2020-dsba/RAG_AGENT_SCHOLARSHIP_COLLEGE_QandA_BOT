# Truck Stuff USA Scholarship

In today’s market, truck drivers are in demand.

As the number of needed truck drivers has increased in recent years, there is a strong demand for trained professionals.

It’s good news for those entering the field because it means salaries are on the rise.

Truck Stuff USA is dedicated to helping trainee truckers earn the skills and qualifications required to thrive in the contemporary trucking industry.

If you would like the training to become a truck driver, there are options to study even if you cannot pay the fees entirely.

Each year, we award a scholarship payment to one applicant to help cover the cost and expenses of the education process.

## Scholarship Amount

The scholarship bursary totals $1,000. It is granted to one applicant per year and must be used to cover learning-related expenses.

## How Will Applications Be Reviewed?

Essays submitted as part of scholarship applications are assessed according to their originality, creativity, and depth of knowledge.

## Privacy Policy for the Scholarship

As part of Truck Stuff USA’s stringent privacy rules, all applicants have a right to privacy during the application process.

No information (names, contact details, written essays) relating to applications is to be shared outside the review team.

No information of any kind is to be passed on to third parties.

Each submission belongs to the submitter and they retain rights over it throughout the application process.
