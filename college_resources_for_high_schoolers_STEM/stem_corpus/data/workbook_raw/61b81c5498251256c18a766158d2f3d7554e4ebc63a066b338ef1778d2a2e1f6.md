# *Live Your Dream Awards*

Soroptimist's *Live Your Dream Awards*program is a unique education award for women who provide the primary financial support for their families. The *Live Your Dream Awards* give women the resources they need to improve their education, skills and employment prospects.

[How to Apply](/our-work/live-your-dream-awards/apply-for-the-live-your-dream-awards.html)   [Support this Program](#FUNRSYMRVUZ)

## What We Do

###### Last year, Soroptimist distributes more than $3.4 million in education awards to over 2,300 women all over the world.

Over half of our *Live Your Dream Awards*recipients are survivors of domestic violence, trafficking or sexual assault. Nearly all of the women and families we serve have overcome enormous obstacles including poverty, teen pregnancy, and drug or alcohol addiction. *Live Your Dream Awards* recipients may use the cash award to offset *any* costs associated with their efforts to attain higher education, such as books, childcare, tuition and transportation.

## Our Impact

###### Since 1972, we've distributed over $49 million in education awards to 47,000 women making a better life for themselves and their families.

We invest in women's education because it transforms lives and communities. The *Live Your Dream Awards* has been recognized for its effectiveness and long-term impact. Past recipients report:

97%

completed their education or have continued working towards their education/training goals

98%

are now living in a safe environment  - of the 57% who are survivors of gendered violence

88%

increased their self-esteem
