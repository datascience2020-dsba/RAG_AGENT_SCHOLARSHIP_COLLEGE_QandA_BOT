# Path to Pro Scholarship Programs

Through the Path to Pro scholarship programs, The Home Depot Foundation has awarded hundreds of scholarships nationwide to students pursuing an education at a trade college or postsecondary program.

* [Path to Pro Scholarship (SkillPointe Foundation)](https://webportalapp.com/sp/login/skillpointefoundation_homedepot-pathtopro)
  + Open to anyone interested in pursuing skilled trades training
  + Applications accepted year-round and approved on a quarterly basis
  + [APPLY HERE](https://webportalapp.com/sp/login/skillpointefoundation_homedepot-pathtopro)

* [Path to Pro Southern California Support Scholarship (SkillPointe Foundation)](https://webportalapp.com/sp/login/skillpointefoundation_homedepot_rebuild-southern-california-scholarship)
  + Open to anyone interested in pursuing skilled trades training, within 60 miles of Los Angeles, CA
  + Applications accepted year-round and approved on a rolling basis
  + [APPLY HERE](https://webportalapp.com/sp/login/skillpointefoundation_homedepot_rebuild-southern-california-scholarship)

* [Folds of Honor Scholarships](https://foldsofhonor.org/scholarships/)
  + Open to qualifying veterans and military family members
  + The next application window opens Feb. 1, 2026
  + [APPLY HERE](https://foldsofhonor.org/scholarships/)
