# STOKES EDUCATIONAL SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM

## Get the Experience You Need

The Stokes Educational Scholarship Program gives high school seniors a pathway into a career
at the National Security Agency with money for college tuition and fees, paid work experience
over multiple summers and continued employment after college graduation.

We will accept applications starting September 1 for the summer 2027 program. Please check

our job board to apply.
