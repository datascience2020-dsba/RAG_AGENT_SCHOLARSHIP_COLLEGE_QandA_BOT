# Better Termite & Pest Control Scholarship

Investing in Local Students, Building Our Community's Future

![](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/joG8ydQy4U0/hqdefault.jpg)

## About the Scholarship

Better Termite & Pest Control, a family-owned, third-generation business, is proud to support local students pursuing higher education. Education has played a transformative role in our own family's success and business growth.

As first-generation college graduates ourselves, we understand the opportunities education creates and want to extend that support to students in our community who share our passion for making a positive impact.

### Our Mission

We aim to empower students who demonstrate ambition and a commitment to their local communities by pursuing degrees in marketing, business, or communications. Through this scholarship, we hope to reduce financial barriers and enable students to achieve their educational and career goals.
