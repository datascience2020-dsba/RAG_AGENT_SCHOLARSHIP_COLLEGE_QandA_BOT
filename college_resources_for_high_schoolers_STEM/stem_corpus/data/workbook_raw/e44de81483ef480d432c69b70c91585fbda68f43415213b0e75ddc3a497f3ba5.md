## Video Topic for the 2026 The Parrish Car Accident & Personal Injury Law Firm Scholarship

Earning a college degree can provide the skills and tools you need to make the world a better place and help others. This year’s video topic asks applicants to explore their plans for the future.

All applicants should include a 2-3-minute video on the following topic with their application:

### Describe how you plan to use your higher education to help others, innovate, and change the world.

When you’re finished with your video, please upload it to your YouTube account (unlisted) and add a link to your scholarship application form.

## Video Requirements

Keep the following criteria in mind as you record your video:

You are permitted to use any media editing tools such as Adobe Creative Cloud to add images, effects, and any desired media elements to your video. The Parrish Car Accident & Personal Injury Law Firm will award our scholarship based on the quality of your script and not your video editing skills. Please take your time to thoughtfully answer the video prompt and tell your story.

Any use of AI, aside from generating B-roll footage is not permitted. Using AI to automatically generate videos or using fake actors is not allowed and will result in the automatic disqualification of your application.
