# Flagship Scholarship

The Point Foundation Flagship Scholarship empowers LGBTQ+ and ally students who are pursuing undergraduate, graduate, or professional degrees at accredited, not-for-profit colleges and universities in the United States by offering financial support, leadership and career development, and community resources.

The Point Foundation Flagship Scholarship empowers LGBTQ+ and ally students who are pursuing undergraduate, graduate, or professional degrees at accredited, not-for-profit colleges and universities in the United States by offering financial support, leadership and career development, and community resources.

### Scholarship Details

Flagship Scholarship recipients receive financial support, access to leadership development programs, and the support of a community of scholars and alumni.

**Amount:** Awards up to $15,000.

**Funding Period:** Awarded Scholars will start their scholarship in the fall 2027 term, with a maximum of four years of support.

### Application Timeline

**Application Opens:** September 9, 9:00 a.m. PST

**Application Closes:** November 19, 5:00 p.m. PST

### Scholarship Eligibility

* A minimum cumulative GPA of 3.3.
* Full-time enrollment in a degree-seeking undergraduate, graduate, or professional program at an accredited, not-for-profit college or university in the United States.
* A member of the LGBTQ+ community or an ally.
* If previously awarded a Point Scholarship, applicants must have exited their former program with active status, which grants eligibility for future scholarships and awards.
* Must be at least a senior in high school at the time of application.

### Scholarship Ineligibility

* Students enrolled in 100% online degree programs.
* Students who have already been awarded four years of the Point Foundation Flagship Scholarship.
* Applicants and their immediate family members who have had a formal relationship with Point Foundation as an employee, board member, or volunteer are not eligible to apply.

## Beyond Financial Support

Selection as a Point Scholar means more than receiving financial support for your college tuition and fees. Point Flagship Scholars have access to programs and resources to help them succeed in their academic journeys.

### Leadership and Career Development

Point offers a variety of leadership and career development opportunities for scholars throughout the year. These opportunities include virtual workshops and in-person events, such as national conferences. Flagship Scholars will attend an in-person summer conference and one virtual program each academic term.

### Community Connections & Networking

With 2,300 LGBTQ+ and ally alumni and supporters ready to help, Point connects scholars with community members with shared areas of study and interests.

## Common Scholarship Submission Questions

Scholars have access to funds at the start of fall term. This scholarship does not support summer study.

The Flagship Scholarship application is open from September through late November. Point does not accept Flagship applications outside of this timeframe. While there is no benefit to early submission, Point highly recommends applicants submit their applications as soon as they have their materials ready. All complete applications submitted by the deadline will receive consideration.

Prior applicants do not have access to past application materials. You must create a new user profile and application to re-apply this year. Previous applications for Point Scholarships have no impact on the review of current applications.

For all other questions and support, please email **[applications@pointfoundation.org](mailto:applications@pointfoundation.org).**
