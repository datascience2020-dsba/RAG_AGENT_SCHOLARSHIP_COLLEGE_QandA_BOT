## **About L. Patrick Laing**

**L. Patrick Laing** served his community with perseverance, dedication and loyalty. Pat Laing was at Fairfax High School for 34 years, serving as director of student activities since 1982 and was for many years the director of the Group AAA Northern Region baseball. As director of student activities, Pat Laing was devoted to Fairfax High School, hiring coaches, scheduling competitions, determining eligibility, lining fields, running tournaments, coordinating sports banquets, getting scholarships for his athletes, and resolving concerns of parents, students, and coaches. Pat Laing cared about his community and especially the students in it.
